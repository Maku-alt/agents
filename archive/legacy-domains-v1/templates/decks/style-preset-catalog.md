# PPT Style Preset Catalog

Curated preset library for deck design exploration.

## How To Use

- Use presets as starting points for `style-discovery`.
- Do not apply them mechanically.
- Translate each preset into PowerPoint behavior:
  - title treatment
  - body typography
  - chart style
  - table style
  - cover behavior
  - closing-slide behavior
- Keep at least one safe editorial option, one bold option, and one wildcard option in every comparison set.

## Recommended Presets For PPT

### 1. Bold Signal

- mood: confident, high-impact, executive
- best_for: strategy, transformation, business updates with strong takeaways
- ppt_translation:
  - dark base with one accent card color
  - large section numbers
  - strong title blocks
  - restrained charts with one highlight accent
- risk:
  - can become too heavy if every slide uses the same card composition

### 2. Electric Studio

- mood: bold, clean, professional, high-contrast
- best_for: executive storytelling, product or capability narratives, quote-led sections
- ppt_translation:
  - split-panel slides for covers or section breaks
  - confident white/dark contrast
  - sharp accent bars for emphasis
- risk:
  - overuse of split panels can reduce flexibility on dense analytical slides

### 3. Dark Botanical

- mood: elegant, premium, reflective
- best_for: premium narratives, leadership messages, high-level vision decks
- ppt_translation:
  - dark editorial slides
  - warm serif display with calm sans body
  - abstract gradients or blurred shapes for soft emphasis
- risk:
  - weak fit for highly operational or table-dense decks

### 4. Notebook Tabs

- mood: editorial, tactile, organized
- best_for: structured explainers, workshop materials, multi-section narratives
- ppt_translation:
  - paper-like light background
  - tab system for sections
  - strong page metaphor without becoming skeuomorphic
- risk:
  - can feel too playful for severe board or risk committees

### 5. Pastel Geometry

- mood: approachable, organized, modern
- best_for: internal education, capability overviews, less formal business reviews
- ppt_translation:
  - soft background plus white content surface
  - vertical pills or side markers for navigation
  - rounded boxes and light shadows used sparingly
- risk:
  - can lose authority if the content is very hard-edged or crisis-oriented

### 6. Vintage Editorial

- mood: witty, personality-driven, editorial
- best_for: thought leadership, trend decks, innovation narratives
- ppt_translation:
  - serif display + clean sans body
  - cream editorial background
  - geometric accents and bordered callouts
- risk:
  - not ideal for highly technical KPI-heavy decks

### 7. Swiss Modern

- mood: precise, rigorous, design-forward
- best_for: analytical storytelling, KPI decks, decision memos with strong structure
- ppt_translation:
  - visible grid discipline
  - black/white base with one red accent
  - asymmetry used carefully
- risk:
  - if poorly executed, it can feel severe rather than elegant

### 8. Paper & Ink

- mood: literary, thoughtful, composed
- best_for: insight decks, narrative readouts, reflective strategic stories
- ppt_translation:
  - warm light background
  - elegant serif moments for section or quote slides
  - horizontal rules and pull-quote behavior
- risk:
  - needs tight editing to avoid feeling too soft

## Presets To Use Sparingly

### Creative Voltage

- good_for: youth, marketing, concept, or innovation-heavy decks
- caution:
  - too much neon energy can undermine credibility in formal business reviews

### Neon Cyber

- good_for: tech demos, AI concepts, speculative futures
- caution:
  - use mostly as a wildcard, not as the default recommendation

### Terminal Green

- good_for: developer, security, command-line, or engineering-facing decks
- caution:
  - highly niche; should rarely be the main executive direction

### Split Pastel

- good_for: lighter internal storytelling or creative team materials
- caution:
  - can feel casual for heavy-risk or finance narratives

## Presets Usually Best Avoided As Default

- highly purple AI-generic directions
- fully centered hero-only layouts
- over-illustrated corporate slides
- glassmorphism-heavy patterns
- default system-font corporate decks

## Suggested Mapping By Deck Type

- board or executive decision deck:
  - Bold Signal
  - Electric Studio
  - Swiss Modern

- premium leadership or vision deck:
  - Dark Botanical
  - Paper & Ink
  - Vintage Editorial

- structured explainer or workshop deck:
  - Notebook Tabs
  - Pastel Geometry

- technical analytical deck:
  - Swiss Modern
  - Electric Studio
  - Bold Signal
